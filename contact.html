How to reach

Come out of Kalkaji Mandir metro station from the mandir side exit and walk up the road towards the gate. We are about two minutes on foot, on the left, after the prasad and chunri shops. Blue tarpaulin, steel buckets outside.

If you are driving, park near the mandir parking - there is no parking right in front of the stall.
