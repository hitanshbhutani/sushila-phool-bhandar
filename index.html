What we make

Rates here are for normal days. On Sawan Somvar, Navratri and in shaadi season the mandi rate goes up and ours goes up also - please call and confirm before you come from far.

Mogra gajra

The everyday one. Small white jasmine tied on cotton thread. Strung between 5 and 6 in the morning.

₹30 per haath

Bela gajra

Bigger flower, much stronger smell. Only in summer, and most days it finishes by afternoon.

₹40 per haath

Chameli gajra

Thin and light. Ladies going to office usually take this one because it does not feel heavy in the hair.

₹40 per haath

Veni (juda phool)

Made to order. Mogra with rose or rajnigandha worked into it, for shaadi, sangeet and roka.

₹80 onwards

Genda mala

Marigold garland. Goes for pooja, for photos of elders, and for tying on the car.

₹40 each

Rajnigandha

Sold in bunches of ten sticks. Keep them in water and they open over three or four days.

₹120 per bunch
