Things people ask

How early should I order for a shaadi?

Two days is the minimum. In November, December and February - which is the busy shaadi season here - please tell us a week before, otherwise the mandi will not have enough mogra held for us.

Do you deliver?

For orders above ₹500 we deliver free in Kalkaji, Govindpuri, CR Park and Nehru Place. Beyond that we can send it by auto but the auto fare has to be paid by you. Small counter items like one gajra you will have to come and take.

How long will a gajra stay fresh?

One day, that is all. Wrap it in newspaper, sprinkle a little water, and keep it in the fridge - the normal part, not the freezer. Do not keep it in a plastic bag tied shut, it turns brown.

Can I get a flower that is not on the list?

Tell us one day before. If it is in season Rakesh will get it from Ghazipur. Orchid, lily and such things we can arrange but the rate is much higher.

Will I get a bill?

We give a hand written bill with the stall stamp, which most offices accept for reimbursement. We are not GST registered so a GST invoice is not possible.

Do you take online payment?

Yes, UPI at the stall. For advance on a big order we can send a UPI request on WhatsApp. We do not take card.
